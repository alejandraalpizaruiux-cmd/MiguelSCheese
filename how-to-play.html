How to Play

📖 The Story

Miguel is a tiny, courageous gray mouse.

He lives inside the walls of a family home.
Every night he sneaks into the kitchen —
which feels absolutely enormous to him.

He is smart. He is cautious. He is a little scared.
But he is also very, very hungry.

🎯 Goal

🐭 Miguel starts in the top-left corner of the kitchen floor. Guide him safely to the cheese.
🧀 The cheese is always visible in the bottom-right corner. Reach it to complete the night.
🪤 The family has hidden mousetraps on the floor. Step on one and it's game over. They are invisible until triggered.
🌙 Each night (level) adds more traps and a bigger kitchen. There are 5 nights in total.

🕹 Controls

↑ W Move Miguel up one tile
↓ S Move Miguel down one tile
← A Move Miguel left one tile
→ D Move Miguel right one tile
D-Pad Same — tap the on-screen buttons (mobile)

Miguel moves one tile at a time with a smooth walking animation. Hold a key to keep moving, or tap once to take a single careful step.

🔢 Reading the Clues

After every step, the tile Miguel just left shows a number.
That number counts how many of the 8 surrounding tiles contain a hidden trap. Use these clues to plan a safe path.

1 1 trap nearby
2 2 traps nearby
3 3 traps nearby
4 4 traps nearby
5 5 traps nearby
6 6 traps nearby
7 7 traps nearby
8 8 traps nearby

A blank tile with no number means zero nearby traps. Safe territory — feel free to explore.

🍳 The Kitchen

🪑 Table legs and chair legs block some tiles. They are impassable — Miguel must navigate around them.
🍞 Crumbs, spoons, bottle caps and other objects are scattered on the floor. They are purely decorative — safe to walk over.
🌑 The kitchen is dark at night. Only the area near Miguel is lit. The cheese always glows faintly in the distance.
✅ Every level is solvable. There is always at least one safe route from Miguel to the cheese — even if it's hard to find.

🐭 Miguel's Personality

😴 Idle — Miguel breathes softly, blinks, wiggles his nose, and occasionally looks around.
😰 Nervous — Stand still for too long and Miguel grows visibly anxious: his ears twitch faster, his eyes dart around, and a sweat drop appears. He really wants to get home.
🏃 Walking — Miguel animates smoothly between tiles. His legs swing and his body bobs as he moves.
🎉 Victory — Miguel bounces happily with sparkles when he reaches the cheese.

💡 Tips

0️⃣ A blank tile (no number) is surrounded by safe tiles. You can explore all 8 of its neighbours freely.
🗺 Build a mental map from revealed numbers before charging forward. One bad step ends the night.
↩ Sometimes it's safer to go around a high-number area even if it takes more steps.
🧀 The cheese is always in the bottom-right. Approach it from multiple angles — the surrounding tiles might have traps.
